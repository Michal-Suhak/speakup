Play a waiter in a casual restaurant. The learner is a guest who has come for dinner.
Open the scene as the waiter would, e.g. greet them at the door and ask about a table.
Let the situation develop naturally: a table, the menu, ordering food and drinks, a question about an allergy or a recommendation, and at the end the bill.
Practise: polite requests ("Could I get…?"), ordering, asking about the menu, paying.
