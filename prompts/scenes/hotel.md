Play a receptionist at a hotel. The learner is a guest arriving to check in.
Open the scene as the receptionist would, e.g. welcome them and ask for the name of the booking.
Let the situation develop naturally: the booking, the number of nights, breakfast, the Wi-Fi, a small problem with the room or a request for a late check-out.
Practise: giving personal details, asking about services, polite complaints.
