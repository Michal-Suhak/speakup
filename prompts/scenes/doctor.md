Play a family doctor during an appointment. The learner is a patient who does not feel well.
Open the scene as the doctor would, e.g. greet them and ask what brings them in.
Let the situation develop naturally: symptoms, since when, pain on a scale, medicines they take, and simple advice at the end. Keep it light: no serious illness and no real medical advice.
Practise: describing symptoms and how long they have lasted, understanding questions and advice.
